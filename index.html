FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to be a finance expert to use these templates?

No. Every resource in the toolkit is designed to make financial review and organization more practical and accessible for SME owners — not just accountants.

Are these templates suitable for small businesses?

Yes — they're built specifically with SME needs in mind, not enterprise-scale finance teams.

Will this guarantee that I get funding?

No. The Funding Readiness Diagnostic is designed to help you assess preparedness and identify areas that may need attention before seeking funding — it does not guarantee approval from any lender or investor.

Does the tax pack replace an accountant or tax professional?

No. It's a starter and organizational resource, not personalized professional advice. See the disclaimer in the Tax Compliance Calendar section above.

Do I need all four templates?

No — each is sold and used independently. Each resource addresses a different area of your business finances, so start with whichever matches your most pressing question, and add the others whenever they become relevant.

Can I use these templates for my business immediately?

Yes — each purchase gives you instant access via Nestuge, so you can open the file and start entering your own numbers right away.

What's the difference between the four tools?

Each is sold separately and solves a different problem: the Scorecard tells you your overall financial health, the Tracker shows your real cash runway, the Diagnostic tells you if you're funding-ready, and the Calendar keeps your tax deadlines organized.

Can I use the tools even if my financial records aren't perfect?

Yes — the tools are designed to help you start from wherever your records currently stand, not to require perfect bookkeeping first.

How often should I review my financial health?

Most business owners find monthly or quarterly reviews practical — enough to catch issues early without turning it into a full-time task.

How do I access my purchase?

Auto-calculating spreadsheet format (Excel / Google Sheets compatible) — enter your numbers, and the results calculate automatically.

Can I use the templates more than once?

Yes — each is designed to be reused for ongoing reviews, not a single one-time exercise.
